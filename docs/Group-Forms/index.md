Group Forms is an extension of the CHEFS platform that enables tenant-scoped form management. It works in close integration with CSTAR (Connected Services, Team Access, and Roles), which is access and role management system that governs who has access to what, and with what roles.

In the Group Forms model, forms are organized under tenants. A tenant is a dedicated workspace for a ministry, team, or initiative. Access to forms within a tenant is controlled by group memberships and service roles managed in CSTAR.

## Core features

All the core form-building and submission features from My Forms are fully available in Group Forms. This includes the form designer, versioning, components, submission management, notifications, integrations, and data export etc. Group Forms adds a tenant-based layer on top of those features.

For getting started with core CHEFS features, see the [Getting started and support](../About#getting-started-and-support) section on the About page.
